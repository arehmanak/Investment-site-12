Shariah Compliance Framework

How Shariah Screening Works

A screening framework helps investors avoid prohibited sectors and filter out financially non-compliant companies. Here is a simplified overview of how stocks and funds are evaluated.

🚫

Business Activity Filter

Review the company's core business and avoid prohibited industries — including alcohol, tobacco, pork, gambling, conventional banking, insurance, and weapons.

📉

Debt Ratio Filter

Check debt and interest-related financial ratios. Companies with interest-bearing debt exceeding 25–33% of total assets are typically excluded.

🔍

Use Screening Tools

Use tools like Islamicly and Zoya for initial screening of individual stocks. These apps provide compliance ratings and detailed breakdowns.

👁️

Manual Review for Edge Cases

Apply manual review for borderline names or sectors. Some companies may pass automated screens but require deeper review of their actual operations.

♻️

Ongoing Monitoring & Purification

Compliance is not a one-time check. Regularly review holdings as companies change. For any incidental impure income, donate the equivalent fraction to charity (purification).

Risks

Understanding the Key Risks

Every investment carries risk. Understanding these upfront helps you invest with discipline, realistic return expectations, and the right time horizon.

📉 Market Volatility

Equity markets fluctuate — especially mid-cap and small-cap stocks. Short-term drawdowns of 20–30% are normal. A long investment horizon (5+ years) is essential.

💱 Currency Risk

Your returns are in INR but your expenses are in AED or USD. Rupee depreciation reduces effective returns when converting back. This works in your favour if INR strengthens.

⚖️ Regulatory Risk

Changes in Indian or UAE regulations affecting foreign investor access, tax treaties, or repatriation rules can impact your investment strategy.

💧 Liquidity Risk

Smaller or less-traded securities may be difficult to exit quickly at fair prices. Stick to liquid funds and well-traded stocks unless you have expert guidance.

Your Journey

Where to Start — Based on Your Experience Level

The right starting point depends on your experience, how much time you can give to monitoring, and your comfort with risk. Start simple and build complexity gradually.

🌱 Beginner

Start with Ethical Mutual Funds

Begin with one or two Shariah-compliant mutual funds (such as Tata Ethical Fund or UTI Nifty 500 Shariah Index Fund). Set up a monthly SIP, even a small one. Let the fund manager do the work while you learn.

📈 Intermediate

Combine Funds with Direct Stocks

Once comfortable, add a selective basket of screened direct equities to your portfolio. Use Islamicly or Zoya to screen, and focus on quality large-cap names first.

🚀 Advanced

Build a Direct Portfolio with Alternatives

Build a comprehensive direct stock portfolio using your own research. Add smallcases or ethical alternative investments where appropriate. Reassess allocation regularly.
